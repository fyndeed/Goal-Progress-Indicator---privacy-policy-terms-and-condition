Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Goal Progress Indicator is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

1. Overview

This is a fully offline application. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no servers, no cloud services, and no analytics. All data remains on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect: your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, browsing history, or any other personal information. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting tool, no advertising framework, and no tracking mechanism in this app.

3. Data Stored Locally

The app stores the following data locally on your device using a Room (SQLite) database:

  • Goal titles, start dates, end dates, and progress status
  • Log entries (text notes you add to track daily progress)
  • Reminder preferences for each goal
  • App settings and first-run agreement status

This data is used solely to provide the app's functionality and never leaves your device.

4. Permissions Used

This app requests the following Android permissions. Each one is explained below with the exact reason it is needed and how it is used.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)

Why: Required to show daily goal reminders and goal-end alert notifications. Without this, reminders will not work on Android 13 and above.

Scope: Only used when you enable reminders for a goal. No notifications are sent without your explicit opt-in. The notification content is your goal title and progress percentage — no personal data is included.

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Why: Android cancels all scheduled alarms when the device is restarted. Without this permission, all your goal reminders would stop working after every reboot. This permission allows the app to automatically re-schedule your reminders after a restart so you never miss a check-in.

Scope: Only reschedules existing alarms that you have already enabled. Does not access any data, does not send any information, does not run any network calls. Simply re-registers the same alarms that were active before the restart.

SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Why: By default, Android groups alarms into approximate time windows (e.g., "sometime between 7:00–7:15 AM"). This permission allows the app to fire your reminder at the exact time you set (e.g., exactly 7:00 AM), so you get your daily check-in prompt precisely when you want it.

Scope: Used only for goal reminder alarms that you explicitly enable when creating or editing a goal. No background scheduling, no recurring system jobs, no hidden alarms.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE (optional)

Why: In rare cases, Android may kill background processes to free memory. A brief foreground service ensures your notification is delivered even in low-memory situations.

Scope: Does not run continuously. Only activates briefly (a few seconds) to deliver a single notification, then stops immediately. No data processing, no network activity.

5. Internet Access

This app does NOT request or use the INTERNET permission. No data is ever sent to any external server, API, or cloud service. All features work entirely offline.

6. Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any third-party SDKs, libraries, or services. There is no Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, AdMob, or any other third-party code that could collect data.

7. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any data from children or any other users. Since no personal data is collected at all, COPPA and similar regulations are inherently satisfied.

8. Data & Storage

All your goals, progress data, and log entries are stored exclusively on your device using Android's Room (SQLite) database. No data is backed up to the cloud or shared with any external service.

The local database is located in the app's private internal storage directory, which is accessible only to the app itself. No other app or service can access this data without root access to the device.

You can delete all data by uninstalling the app or by deleting individual goals from within the app. Deleting a goal permanently removes all its log entries, progress data, and reminder settings from the local database.

There is no export, backup, or sync feature. Your data stays and dies with the app on your device.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete any individual goal and its associated log entries at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data from your device.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app's functionality. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
